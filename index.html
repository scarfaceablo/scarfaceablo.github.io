About K2 Solutions

K2 Solutions is a custom software solutions company based in Slovenia. We design and build full-stack web applications, data platforms, and AI-powered systems for businesses that need software tailored to how they actually work.

Drawing on more than a decade of combined experience across web development, cloud infrastructure, and data engineering, we help businesses automate critical workflows, integrate disparate systems, and build software that scales with them. From focused MVPs to multi-year enterprise platforms, we deliver solutions that are reliable, maintainable, and built to last.

Our approach combines full-stack development with data engineering and modern AI tooling, letting us deliver complete solutions end-to-end — from data ingestion through to user-facing applications. We work with startups and established companies on engagements ranging from focused integrations to multi-year platforms, leveraging AI-assisted development to ship faster without compromising on quality.
